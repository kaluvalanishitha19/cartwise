# Refunds policy

## How refunds are issued
All refunds go back to the original payment method used at checkout. We cannot
issue refunds to a different card or account.

## Refund timelines
Once a refund is approved, it typically takes 7–10 business days for the money
to appear in your account, depending on your bank. For returned items, the
refund is approved after the warehouse receives and inspects your return.

## Large refunds
Refunds over $150 require a manual review by our support team before they are
issued. This review typically adds 1–2 business days. You will be notified by
email once the review is complete and your refund is approved.

## Partial refunds
If a returned item arrives at the warehouse used, damaged by the customer, or
missing tags or packaging, we may issue a partial refund of up to 50% of the
item price instead of a full refund.

## Cancelled orders
Orders cancelled before shipment are refunded in full automatically — no
review needed, though refunds over $150 still follow the large refund review
process. Refunds for cancelled orders follow the same 7–10 business day
timeline.
