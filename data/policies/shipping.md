# Shipping policy

## Shipping methods and timeframes
Standard shipping takes 6–10 business days from the day your order ships.
Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding holidays. You will receive
a confirmation email with a tracking number as soon as your order ships.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free for orders over $50. Orders of $50 or less ship for
a flat rate of $5.99.

## Delayed packages
Carriers occasionally report delays due to weather or volume. If your tracking
shows a delay, the new estimated delivery date will appear in your order
timeline. A delayed package is not considered lost — see below for when a
package qualifies as lost.

## Lost packages
If your package has not arrived within 20 days after the estimated delivery
date, it is treated as lost. Contact support to open a lost package claim. We
review each claim with the delivery carrier, and once the review is complete
you receive a full refund to your original payment method. Reviews typically
take 3–5 business days.

## Address changes
We can update your shipping address only before the order ships. Once an order
has shipped, the address cannot be changed.
