# Returns policy

## Standard returns
Most items can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date. Items must be
unused, undamaged, and have all original tags and packaging intact. You will
need your order number to start a return. Approved returns are refunded to the
original payment method.

## Clearance items
Clearance items follow the same condition rules but have a shorter window:
they must be returned within 7 days of the delivery date. After 7 days,
clearance purchases cannot be returned or exchanged.

## Final sale items
Items marked "Final sale" cannot be returned, exchanged, or refunded under any
circumstances. This is separate from clearance — check the product page:
clearance items say "Clearance", final sale items say "Final sale".

## Damaged or defective items
If your item arrives damaged or defective, report it within 7 days of delivery
through the returns portal. We may ask for a photo of the damage. You will
receive a full refund or a free replacement your choice and for clearly
damaged items you do not need to ship the item back. Damage caused after
delivery (drops, spills, normal wear) is not covered.

## How to start a return
Start your return in the returns portal using your order number and email.
Once approved, we email you a prepaid FedEx or UPS shipping label. Refunds
are issued after the warehouse receives and inspects the item see our
refunds policy for timelines and details.
